Create stunning digital business cards with QR codes. Share your contact info instantly — no paper needed. Design personalized vCards with your name, phone, email, and social links, then generate a scannable QR code to share with anyone.

Features:
• Create beautiful digital contact cards
• Generate QR codes for instant sharing
• Store multiple card designs
• Customize colors and layouts
• Export and share via any messaging app
• Works offline — no internet required to display cards

Perfect for networking events, business meetings, and everyday connections. Go paperless with Digital QR Cards.
