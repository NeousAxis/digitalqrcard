Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a digital business card?

Tap the "+" button on the main screen, fill in your contact details, choose a theme, and save. Your QR code is generated automatically.

How do I share my card?

Open your card and tap the share or QR code button. You can share via QR code scan, or download the QR image to send digitally.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete individual cards from within the app. For complete account deletion, please contact us via email.

My cards are not syncing

Make sure you are logged in with the same account on all devices. Cards sync automatically via cloud storage when you have an internet connection.
